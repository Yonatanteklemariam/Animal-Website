Cat

Cats are small, carnivorous mammals that are often kept as pets. They are known for their agility, playful behavior, and ability to hunt vermin. Cats have been associated with humans for thousands of years and are one of the most popular pets worldwide.

Interesting Facts About Cats

  • Cats have retractable claws that help them climb and catch prey.
  • They are known for their grooming habits and spend a significant amount of time cleaning themselves.
  • Cats can make over 100 different sounds, while dogs can only make about 10.
  • They have excellent night vision, allowing them to see in low light conditions.
  • Cats are known for their independence but can also form strong bonds with their owners.
